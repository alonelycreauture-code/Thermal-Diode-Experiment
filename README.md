# Thermal-Diode-Experiment
Science fair project that was made in group, is subject to change right now

# First Trial



Observations:
	During the first trial, sensor 3 increased noticeably after 12 seconds, having an increase greater than 40°C, which meant that the aluminum plate was successfully conducting heat from the boiling water, or our heating element. The aluminum on the other plate remained cool due to the fan being positioned over it. In the end, throughout the trial, the paraffin wax remained in the middle, absorbing heat while the other plate was being cooled on its own.

The thermal diode maintained conductivity while it periodically cooled down.



Sensor Layout (What each sensor measured): 

- Sensor 1:  Paraffin(Wax)
- Sensor 2: (Cold side of thermal diode/Fan)
- Sensor 3: (Boiling Water)

# Second Trial



Observations:
	A few things were improved for the second trial, most importantly, a thinner layer of wax was used. When the trial started, the aluminum slowly started to absorb the heat, which meant that the aluminum plate was successfully conducting the heat from the heating source. The aluminum on the other plate remained cool due to the fan being positioned over it, although the paraffin was actually retaining more heat than in the last trial. In the end, throughout the trial, the paraffin wax remained in the middle, absorbing heat while the other plate was being cooled on its own.

The thermal diode maintained better heat conductivity while it periodically cooled down.


Sensor Layout (What each sensor measured): 

- Sensor 1:  (Cold side of aluminum)
- Sensor 2: (Hot side of aluminum)
- Sensor 3: (Paraffin Wax)
